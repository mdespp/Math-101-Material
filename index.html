Multiplication & Factors

Start with multiplication, then learn factors. Move on to GCF and LCM when you feel ready.

Multiplication

Multiplying two integers gives their product. For example, 3 × 4 = 12.

Open the multiplication table (1–12)
3 × 4 = 12

Choose a cell to see the multiplication fact. Scroll sideways on a small screen.

How do negative signs work?

The same signs give a positive product: (−3) × (−4) = 12. Different signs give a negative product: (−3) × 4 = −12.

Factors & pairs

A factor divides a number evenly. A factor pair is two integers whose product is that number. For 12, one pair is 3 and 4.

Explore the factors of a number

GCF & LCM

GCF (also called GCD) is the greatest positive factor shared by two numbers. For 12 and 18, it is 6.

A multiple is obtained by multiplying a number by an integer. LCM is the least positive multiple shared by two numbers. For 12 and 18, it is 36.
